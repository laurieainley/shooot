Is it free?

Yes, while we’re in early access. We’ll tell you well before anything changes.

What camera do I need?

Whatever you’ve got. GoPros and phones both work: anything that records a normal video file.

Do I need to edit video?

No. You tap when something happens; Shooot does the cutting, replays and graphics.

Who sees my footage?

Only the people you share the reel with.
