Video smarts

TV-style team intros

Open the reel like a broadcast: each team's five players appear in their kit, arms folded or stepping towards the camera, with the team name. Then the other team. All from one photo of each player.

MMedium for still cut-outs, Large with real movement. Cutting players out of their photos and animating them in can run in the browser, so photos stay on the device. Folded arms or a walk to camera needs an AI video model, either open-source or a paid service. A paid service means sending players' faces off the device, so each player's consent comes first. Each clip is made once and reused every week.
